# Lucky Buck Ticket 1.0.0 — balloons, aiming and prize numbers (November 8, 2026)

Owner approved going live ("Yes put it live") after trying the preview on their iPhone and tuning the settings.

Files changed: `ticket-1.0.0.html` and `playtest.html` (identical). `index.html` (calculator, v3.3.3) is unchanged.

## What changed
- **Balloons (owner pick: option D):** new hand-built pixel-art balloons in red, blue and yellow. Round bodies, glossy upper-left shine, squeaky rim glow, and a black permanent-marker X. The X's centre cell is the exact centre of the image, which is the point the game measures hits and bullseyes from (checked to within 0.01 px). The centre pixel shows the balloon's own colour. The string now starts under the knot.
- **Popped balloons disappear completely.** The old pop animation froze at 85% visible; fixed.
- **Crosshair (owner pick: Hunter orange shape, in red):** a 17-cell pixel crosshair on the same grid as the balloons, so its single centre pixel is the same size as a balloon's centre pixel.
- **Aiming:**
  - The crosshair floats above the finger so the thumb doesn't hide the target.
  - While held, it traces an infinity sign, then a figure 8, then repeats (2.4 s per shape, seamless hand-off). This replaces the random wobble.
- **Prize numbers (owner pick: B):**
  - Press Start 2P pixel digits, coloured by prize: $25 gold, $5 green, $1 blue, $0 red.
  - Each number bounces in with a burst of eight pixel stars as the balloon pops.
  - This replaces the rounded dark number boxes.
- **Owner-tuned defaults:** balloon speed 1.6×, movement distance 100%, crosshair pattern size 20 px, crosshair height above finger 130 px. Sliders remain under Testing controls.

## Not changed
- Odds: 75% lose, 17% $1, 6.5% $5, 1.5% $25 (fixed in the ticket; not linked to the simulator).
- Hit radius, accuracy maths, outcomes, sounds and the calculator.
- The ticket's testing controls are not shared with the simulator. Linking the two would be a separate task needing owner approval.

## Tests
- Browser emulation at iPhone width: $25 winner, $5 winner, $1 winner and losers played; flip to rules and back; no page errors.
- Crosshair and balloon cell sizes match (2.292 px each at 390 px wide), with 0 px centre offset.
- Owner tried the preview on a real iPhone (screenshots shared) before approving. The press-and-hold feel was tuned by the owner.
